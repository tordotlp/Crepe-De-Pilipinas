A little about us

Our roots are local.
Our crepes are special.

Crepe de Pilipinas is a student business bringing together the fun of French crepes and the familiar flavors of the Philippines. We want every bite to feel a little special while staying friendly to a student's budget.

Created for our school community at La Salle College Antipolo, our goal is simple: share a sweet treat with a Filipino twist.
